# Author portrait credits

All portraits were sourced from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to a 4:5 portrait ratio, resized to 720 × 900 pixels, and converted to WebP for the Orphaleia author directory. Archival monochrome portraits were color-restored with AI assistance while preserving their original composition; the resulting colors are interpretive rather than documentary evidence.
